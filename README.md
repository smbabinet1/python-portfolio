# python-portfolio
this is where I store all of my Python Projects from the semester

**Cat**
**Summary**: This project is a game that takes inputs from the player to determine what famous cartoon cat they are.

**Key Features**: 
- Each answer you give impacts which character the game will give you.

**Multiplication Quiz**
**Summary**: This is a multiplication quiz. The player answers random multiplication questions and gets a score.

**Key Features**: 
- Timer tells you how long you spent on the quiz
- You may choose how many questions you answer
  
**Pokemon App**
**Summary**: This is a pokemon evolution game. Train, battle, and rest your pokemon and watch it evolve.

**Key Features**: 
- Continue to play until you choose to exit
- Displays Pokemon level and appearance whenever you rest or evolve.

**Rock Paper Scissors**
**Summary**: A game of rock paper scissors against the computer.

**Key Features**: 
- Generates your move
- Generates computer's move
- Compares scores
- Continue until you choose not to

**Simple Calculator**
**Summary**: A simple calculator that can add, subtract, multiply, and divide numbers given by the user.

**Key Features**: 
- Option to Quit
- Performs math operations with numbers given by the user
